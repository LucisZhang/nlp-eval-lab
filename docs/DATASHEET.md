## 1. Motivation

This datasheet documents the frozen dataset underlying a three-tier consumer-complaint triage evaluation lab. The task is to predict a stable routing class from a consumer's free-text complaint narrative, and to measure how model quality and routing cost degrade under real 2015→2026 distribution drift. The dataset is a single frozen snapshot of the public CFPB Consumer Complaint Database; no new data was collected and no consumers were contacted.

### 2.1 Privacy & scrubbing

Consumer narratives are **opt-in**: they appear only when the complainant affirmatively consented to publication. Before publication, **CFPB scrubs the narratives and masks personally identifiable information as the literal token `XXXX`** (variable-length runs such as `XXXX`/`XXXXXXXX` denote redacted spans). **This lab performed NO redaction, de-identification, or PII removal of its own** — it consumes the already-scrubbed CFPB text verbatim and makes no claim about the completeness of CFPB's masking. The dedup normalizer does collapse runs of the `x` mask to a single token for near-duplicate detection only; it never rewrites the stored narrative.

## 4. Preprocessing

### 4.1 Taxonomy harmonization

CFPB renamed and merged its `Product` taxonomy several times over 2015–2026 (notably the 2017-04 revision and the 2023 credit-reporting consolidation). To keep the routing target stable across the temporal splits, historical `Product` values are collapsed into fixed routing **classes** by the frozen `taxonomy_map.yaml`. Every mapping decision and its rationale is reproduced below verbatim from that file; changing any mapping invalidates all downstream splits and results. The taxonomy churn itself is retained as a measured "label-drift" exhibit rather than hidden.

Taxonomy map version: **1**.

#### `card`

Historical `Product` values mapped here:

- 'Credit card'
- 'Credit card or prepaid card'
- 'Prepaid card'

*Rationale.* The 2017-2023 merged label "Credit card or prepaid card" (108k rows) is unsplittable at Product level, so all card-like instruments collapse together; keeping prepaid separate would give the same narratives different labels in different eras. Crosses B1 (merge) and B2 (split-back). Known impurity: stored-value prepaid products mixed with revolving credit.

#### `credit_reporting`

Historical `Product` values mapped here:

- 'Credit reporting'
- 'Credit reporting, credit repair services, or other personal consumer reports'
- 'Credit reporting or other personal consumer reports'
- 'Debt or credit management'

*Rationale.* The three credit-reporting names are one concept renamed at B1 and B2. "Debt or credit management" (2023+ carve-out: credit repair, debt settlement, counseling) folds here for TRAIN continuity: the 2017-2023 label explicitly included "credit repair services", so pre-2023 models learned these narratives as credit reporting. Alternative considered and rejected: debt_collection (TRAIN never saw these as third-party collection). Known impurity: post-2023 debt-settlement/counseling complaints sit here rather than in debt_collection. This mapping is the flagship label-drift exhibit.

#### `debt_collection`

Historical `Product` values mapped here:

- 'Debt collection'

*Rationale.* Single stable label across 2015-2026; no era crossing. Deliberately does NOT absorb "Debt or credit management" (see credit_reporting).

#### `deposit_account`

Historical `Product` values mapped here:

- 'Bank account or service'
- 'Checking or savings account'

*Rationale.* Predecessor/successor rename at B1. Known impurity: the pre-2017 label was slightly broader than checking/savings.

#### `money_service`

Historical `Product` values mapped here:

- 'Money transfers'
- 'Virtual currency'
- 'Money transfer, virtual currency, or money service'

*Rationale.* The post-2017 label explicitly enumerates both old labels, so this is a documented merge. Crosses B1.

#### `mortgage`

Historical `Product` values mapped here:

- 'Mortgage'

*Rationale.* Single stable label across 2015-2026; no era crossing.

#### `payday_personal_loan`

Historical `Product` values mapped here:

- 'Payday loan'
- 'Payday loan, title loan, or personal loan'
- 'Payday loan, title loan, personal loan, or advance loan'

*Rationale.* One successor chain renamed at both B1 and B2 (B2 adds "advance loan"). Kept separate from vehicle_loan because payday/title and auto lending carry very different triage cost profiles.

#### `student_loan`

Historical `Product` values mapped here:

- 'Student loan'

*Rationale.* Single stable label across 2015-2026; no era crossing.

#### `vehicle_loan`

Historical `Product` values mapped here:

- 'Vehicle loan or lease'
- 'Consumer Loan'

*Rationale.* At B1 the old "Consumer Loan" parent split into vehicle and payday/personal successors. "Consumer Loan" (2015-2017, 9,458 rows) folds here on majority-composition grounds (auto dominates CFPB consumer-installment volume). Known impurity: early-TRAIN vehicle_loan contains some personal installment loans. Sensitivity alternative (mapping it to payday_personal_loan) may be run as a separate experiment, never as an in-place edit.

#### Dropped products

- **'Other financial service'** — Pre-2017-only residual grab-bag (money orders, check cashing, debt settlement, foreclosure relief; 292 rows, 0.008% of corpus) with no coherent stable successor. Extinct before every test split, so dropping costs no test coverage; folding it anywhere would inject heterogeneous noise. Only product dropped.

### 4.2 Near-duplicate removal (dedup)

CFPB narratives contain large volumes of near-duplicate template complaints (credit-report disputes especially). Near-duplicates are removed **before** splitting so that resubmitted or templated complaints cannot leak across the train/test boundary. Detection uses MinHash + LSH banding (datasketch): each narrative is normalized (lowercased, mask runs collapsed, non-alphanumerics stripped), shingled into word 5-grams, and signed with a fixed-seed 128-permutation MinHash; signatures are banded and union-found into near-duplicate clusters. Per cluster the earliest-dated record (tie-break: smallest complaint id) is kept and the rest dropped. The procedure is deterministic across runs and worker counts.

- **Input rows (pre-dedup):** 3,830,206
- **Output rows (post-dedup):** 1,983,496
- **Removed rows:** 1,846,710
- **Dedup rate:** 0.4821 (48.21% of input rows removed)
- **Near-duplicate clusters:** 1,983,496
- **MinHash/LSH parameters:** num_perm=128, minhash_seed=1, hashfunc=sha1_hash32, shingle_size=5, num_bands=8, rows_per_band=16, lsh_threshold=0.9, datasketch=2.0.0

## 6. Uses

The dataset supports supervised text classification (narrative → routing class), model calibration, confidence-cascade router evaluation against an explicit cost model, and temporal drift / prior-shift measurement across the frozen TEST-DRIFT and TEST-POSTCUTOFF slices. **Not suitable** for: any attempt to re-identify individuals (the text is opt-in and CFPB-scrubbed, and re-identification is out of scope and discouraged); demographic or fairness auditing (no demographic labels are present); or treating `Product` as ground-truth consumer intent (it is the company/CFPB routing category, harmonized here across taxonomy revisions).
